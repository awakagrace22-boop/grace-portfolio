6+years where creativity meets business

For over six years, I've been exploring the space where creativity meets business.

From building brand identities and designing packaging to developing campaigns, creating content and shaping communications, I enjoy taking an idea from that initial spark to something people can actually see, experience and connect with.

I'm currently a Brand & Communications Designer at HACO Industries, Nairobi, working across a portfolio of FMCG brands including AMARA, MIADI, SO-SOFT, ACE and Ashanti-Q.

My work moves between shelves, screens and stories. And honestly, I quite like it that way.

The way I think

I design for more than the first impression.

Good design has a job to do.

Whether it's a product label, a campaign or a social media visual, I believe creativity should have purpose. It should communicate something, solve something or make someone feel something.

I think beyond the brief.

I like understanding the bigger picture. The audience, the business objective, the production realities and the little details that can make or break an idea.

I enjoy connecting the dots.

Design doesn't exist in isolation. It connects with marketing, communication, consumer behaviour and business strategy. I enjoy bringing those disciplines together.

I'm always learning, experimenting and making.

From exploring 3D product visualisation to experimenting with AI-assisted creative workflows and digging into marketing analytics, I'm interested in what becomes possible when curiosity meets technology.

About me

Creative by nature. Strategic by curiosity.
