Behind the label

I'm Grace Awaka Otsimi, a brand and communications designer who has spent six-plus years helping FMCG brands get noticed, picked up and remembered.

From intern to the person who looks after five brands

I started at HACO Industries as a graphic design intern in 2018, editing images and prepping files. Today I lead brand, packaging and digital work across Amara, Miadi, SoSoft, ACE, Ashanti-Q and the HACO corporate brand.

That means packaging renovations and launches, campaign visual systems, social pages, corporate catalogues and newsletters, and keeping agencies, regulators, sales and suppliers pointed at the same idea.

I care about work that looks good and does its job: clearer packs, faster approvals, bigger audiences. Right now I'm also deepening the marketing side with a Diploma in Digital Marketing at Zetech University.
