Hi, I'm Grace. A creative mind with a strategic streak.

And an unhealthy curiosity for how things are made. For over six years, I've been exploring the space where creativity meets business. From building brand identities and designing packaging to developing campaigns, creating content and shaping communications, I enjoy taking an idea from that initial spark to something people can actually see, experience and connect with.

I'm currently a Brand & Communications Designer at HACO Industries, Nairobi, working across a portfolio of FMCG brands including AMARA, MIADI, SO-SOFT, ACE and Ashanti-Q. My work moves between shelves, screens and stories. And honestly, I quite like it that way.

I'm naturally curious. I like understanding how things work, why people respond to certain ideas and how seemingly unrelated disciplines can come together to create something interesting.
